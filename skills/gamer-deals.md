## When to use it
Buying games (digital or physical), consoles, controllers, headsets or other gaming gear.

## Tips
- Big digital storefronts run large seasonal sales (summer and end of year). If a game is not urgent, put it in the wallet with a target price and wait for the next sale.
- Compare the digital price with physical copies: discs and cartridges often drop in price sooner and can be resold.
- "Deluxe" or "Gold" editions are frequently cheaper than the base game plus its add-ons bought separately, but only if you will actually play the add-ons.
- For consoles, manufacturer-refurbished units usually come with a warranty and cost noticeably less than new.
- Check the region and platform of a key or code before buying: a code for another region or launcher may not work.
- Controllers and headsets: last year's model often drops a lot when a new one launches.

## Red flags
- Third-party key resellers with prices far below every official store: keys can be revoked later.
- "New" consoles from unknown sellers with no warranty or return policy.
- Bundles padded with games you already own.
