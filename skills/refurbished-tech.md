## When to use it
Electronics where last year's or refurbished models are a good deal.

## Tips
- Prefer "certified refurbished" from the manufacturer or a seller that gives at least a 1-year warranty.
- For phones and laptops, ask the seller for battery health; a worn battery can cost a lot to replace.
- Compare the refurbished price with the current new price on sale: sometimes new is only slightly more.
- Headphones and keyboards: open-box items from major retailers usually keep the full return window.

## Red flags
- "Like new" with no warranty and no return window.
- Unlocked phones without a clear statement of carrier and region compatibility.
