## When to use it
Espresso machines, drip and pour-over gear, grinders and coffee accessories.

## Tips
- A good burr grinder improves coffee more than an expensive machine. If the budget is tight, suggest spending on the grinder first.
- Count the running cost, not only the price: pod machines are cheap to buy and expensive per cup.
- Manufacturer-refurbished espresso machines are a common way to save on mid-range models; check the warranty length.
- Check that the machine's steam wand or frother matches what the user drinks (milk drinks vs black coffee).
- Manual pour-over kits are a low-cost way to get very good coffee.

## Red flags
- Blade grinders sold as "espresso grinders".
- Machines with no descaling instructions or spare parts available.
